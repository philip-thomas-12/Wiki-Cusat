# Wiki CUSAT

Wiki CUSAT is a smart, interactive digital platform that represents the entire CUSAT university ecosystem through a campus map, knowledge graph, and community-driven communication system. The platform visualizes CUSAT as a living network of departments, faculty, mentors, alumni, students, projects, placements, facilities, and vendors.

An interactive campus map acts as the core interface, allowing users to explore real locations and instantly access contextual academic, professional, and infrastructural information related to each entity.

A key innovation of Wiki CUSAT is its context-aware communication layer. Each section (faculty, mentors, alumni, projects, placements, facilities, and vendors) includes a dedicated chat space, enabling users to interact anonymously or openly with the relevant community. This promotes hesitation-free knowledge sharing, guidance, and collaboration, while a common campus chat enables cross-community discussions.

To enhance accessibility, Wiki CUSAT integrates an AI-powered chatbot that acts as a virtual campus assistant, helping users navigate the platform and get instant responses related to academics, facilities, placements, and opportunities.

Overall, Wiki CUSAT transforms scattered university information into a single intelligent, interactive, and collaborative ecosystem, improving transparency, connectivity, and engagement across the campus. The platform is scalable and adaptable for other universities as well.
